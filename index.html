About

Where enterprise process meets applied technology

Ivalua Source-to-Pay (S2P) Specialist driving digital procurement transformation within Deloitte India’s Supply Chain & Network Operations practice. Fully certified in Ivalua L3 CLM and Ivalua L3 Sourcing, with expertise in configuring procurement workflows that strengthen compliance, streamline processes, and support commercial value realization. I bring a strong understanding of both Source-to-Contract (S2C) and Procure-to-Pay (P2P) processes across the procurement lifecycle.

My S2C expertise spans sourcing projects, Catalog Management, Item Price Sheets, Punchout Items, RFx Management, competitive awarding, online auctions, multi-round and multi-lot RFx events, and post-event analysis. In P2P, I have hands-on knowledge of the procurement journey from Requisition and Purchase Order through Invoice, Delivery, and Receipt, with experience in P2R process governance, PR/PO workflows, SIT, UAT, user access management, amendments, and supplier verification. I look forward to applying this expertise to drive further impact as a full-time Analyst beginning in January 2027.
